The texts about saints, feasts and historical events are written in our own words and show their verification state; they are not an official church or historical source.

CONTENT AND IMAGES
Images are primarily sourced from Wikimedia Commons and NASA. Their metadata states the declared creator, licence and source. WebP files have undergone format conversion. Declared licences are not independent legal clearance or a blanket grant of third-party rights.
The data rest on public sources: the Megas Synaxaristis (synaxarion.gr) for the memories of the saints and the Greek Wikipedia (el.wikipedia.org) for names and years. Factual verification and review of redistribution rights for texts and databases are separate processes. The latter remains under review.
